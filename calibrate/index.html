Calibrate

With the manager

Log in as a setup tech or an operator, and the manager measures from this phone as you hold it up.

Pre-show calibration check With a stage beacon standing ready, offers the beacon first and walks you only to the cameras it leaves. Takes you to each camera once. You hold this phone in front of it and tap; the manager puts that camera on air with its own microphone and sets its lip sync. Then it times the network cameras, matches the cameras’ colours by what they see (each shown beside the one it is matched to, to keep or leave), and asks where the programme’s sound should come from: one camera, or every microphone joined in time. With the programme mix, it measures the mix’s own timing last: from the stage beacon when it can, else with the flashes. Start with what is due Every camera again Start with what is due passes over a camera the Before the show list has as ready: measured on this stream, or checked or surely set by the stage beacon since. To measure one of those again, use Every camera again, and skip the others.
Pick it up
Calibrate sound Flashes and beeps while the manager measures each camera’s sound against its picture and sets its lip sync. All at once, from one place: hold it where every camera sees and hears it, about 15 seconds (30 with the programme mix). Time the network cameras Flashes, without the beeps, while the manager times each network camera against the phones by the flashes both see, and sets what it is held by in OBS. Hold it still where a network camera and at least one phone camera both see it, about 20 seconds.
Calibrate colour Shows a chart the manager finds in each camera by itself, then fits each camera’s colours. Hold it still, facing the cameras, under the stage lights, about 30 seconds. Held sideways, the chart is half as big again, and easier for a far camera to read: turn the phone before you start.

On their own

Sync flash White flashes and beeps together, for Calibrate sync on the manager’s Sound page: each camera’s sound lined up with its own picture. Colour card A 24-patch colour chart, an 18% grey, white, a grey scale and colour bars, for white balance and the Colour page’s chart matching. Turn the screen’s brightness up, and Night Shift, True Tone or any eye-comfort mode off, so every camera sees the same colours.
