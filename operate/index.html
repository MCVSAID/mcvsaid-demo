Operate

The cameras you choose, live (no look-ahead), steered with an Xbox controller or the buttons. Each gimbal camera’s Positions are set up here too. A network PTZ camera is shown as OBS has it, and aimed and zoomed the same way once its card has its control address.

Cameras shown

Controllers

Buttons
Right stickAim (pan and tilt); gentle near the middle, the further you push, the faster
Right trigger / left triggerZoom in / out: squeeze lightly to creep, fully to move
APut this camera on air
BStop the gimbal (a network camera: stop it turning and zooming)
Right stick pressGo home: back to the home position (set with Set home on its tile); stops following. A network camera goes to its own home
XFollow on or off (a network camera: its own tracker)
YBack to the previous shot
View (⧉)Main stage: the stage shot on air
LB / RB, or d-pad left / rightThe previous / next camera shown here
D-pad up / downFaster / slower aiming (Aim on the bar, 1 to 10; kept on this device)
Menu (≡) held + A / B / X / YGo to position 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 (the Positions under its picture)
Menu (≡) held + left stick pressSave where it points now and its zoom as a new position (Position N; rename it under the picture)
