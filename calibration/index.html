Calibrate

Once the cameras are up and streaming, one person can calibrate them from the stage: open the calibrate page on a phone, log in, and hold it up where the cameras see it. Calibrate sound flashes and beeps while the manager measures each camera’s lip sync; Calibrate colour shows a chart that the manager finds in every camera by itself, fits each camera’s colours and puts the corrections in OBS; with a network camera, Time the network cameras flashes while the manager times it against the phones by the flashes both see, and sets what it is held by in OBS.
